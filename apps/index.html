Video Games, Apps & Tools

Being old-school gamers, whenever we get a little spare time away from making board games, we love tinkering in our digital garden shed. This is our app ideas sandbox: everything here is free to play or use, runs in your browser, and needs no logins or downloads.

Bangers & Smash

A free, classic-car demolition derby racing game that plays right in your browser. No downloads, no login and no account. Just jump in and smash!

BOD3D

A free-to-play, browser-based version of Bag of Dungeon, with no downloads or login. Your quest: get in, grab the Ring, and try to get out alive!

Play BOD3D ↗

E.D.D.i. Reader

E.D.D.i. stands for Electronic Digital Document Intelligence: a free reader that brings books and documents to life with natural-sounding open-source AI voices.

SHRINK 3D

A free STL file and batch reduction optimisation tool for 3D gaming and printing. Shrink, repair, fuse and download!

PrintWorks

A free browser-based PDF preflight tool for checking your files before they go to print. Check CMYK colours, image resolution and print readiness. Check it, fix it, and print it!

Open PrintWorks ↗

Kastles Krackdown

Play 50 free levels of medieval castle mayhem! Fling rocks, collect gems, and bring down the King and Queen. Desktop only!
