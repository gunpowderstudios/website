King of the Kastles

Small box. Big siege.

Build your Kastle, then crush thy neighbour!

Kastles is a fast and furious battling card game where you pit your wits against your enemies. Defend your stronghold while plotting the destruction of theirs.

Every game is different. Use your cards to plan attacks with gunpowder, flaming arrows, lethal catapults and saboteurs — or call on a wizard to command the fearsome dragon.

With simple set-up and play, it’s great for beginners and experienced players alike, whether you’re at home, on holiday or on a long journey.

How to play Kastles.

Build your Kastle, draw cards, play single cards or combinations, and outlast your neighbours.

1

Build your Kastle.

Set up your stronghold and get ready to defend it.

2

Draw your cards.

Draw from the deck and choose your attacks. Play a single card or combine cards for a bigger strike.

3

Crush thy neighbour.

Use catapults, gunpowder, flaming arrows, saboteurs and other tricks to reduce opposing Kastles to rubble.

4

Last Kastle standing.

Keep defending and attacking until yours is the final stronghold left.
