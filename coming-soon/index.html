How would you prepare for an apocalyptic event? What’s top of your looting list? Would you stock up on meds, food? Or hunker down in your hidden bunker?

Can you make your pizza before everyone else and get paid? Forget the quality — as long as you make some dough, even a BAD Pizza will do!

A clever, even more pocket-sized spin-off from Bag of Dungeon. Working solo or cooperatively, 1–4 players must battle monsters, avoid deadly traps and find their way through a twisting labyrinth to recover the Ring and reach the exit.

Planned release: Early 2027
