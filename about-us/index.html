Our story

Less screen time.
More table time.

Independent British board game makers creating big adventures in small boxes.

Welcome to the lo-fi revolution.

Gunpowder Studios is an independent British design studio creating fun, accessible and imaginative board games for the whole family.

Our games are made for everyone — from young adventurers discovering board gaming for the first time to parents and seasoned players searching for their next family favourite.

We believe great games should be easy to learn, exciting to play and ready to take anywhere. Our bestselling Bag of Dungeon packs an entire fantasy adventure into a bag no bigger than a packet of crisps!

Whether you love fantasy adventures, dungeon crawlers or quick strategy games, our mission is simple: bring people together around the table for real adventures, unforgettable moments and plenty of laughter.
